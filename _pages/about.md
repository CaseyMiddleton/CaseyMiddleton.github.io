Hi, I'm Casey! I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Melbourne, working with Drs. Freya Shearer and James McCaw. I'm broadly interested in infectious disease modeling — specifically in building models that are not just theoretically grounded, but genuinely useful for public health decision-making.

---

## Education

<div class="about-callout">
<strong>PhD, Computer Science — University of Colorado Boulder (May 2025)</strong>
Thesis: <em>"Multiscale Infectious Disease Dynamics: Linking Epidemiology and Testing for Outbreak Neutralization"</em> (did you catch the acronym?)
</div>

I worked with [Dr. Dan Larremore](https://larremorelab.github.io/) on combining within-host and between-host models to produce better predictions for infectious disease dynamics. You can read more on my [publications page](/publications/).

**BS, Biomathematics** (minor in Chemistry) — Rhodes College, Memphis, TN

At Rhodes, I got an early start in mathematical modeling through published research on yellow fever outbreak control and predator-prey dynamics between Golden Eagles and Island Foxes. I also loved exploring outside my major — courses in art history, Eastern Asian studies, and religion in American culture were some of my favorites.

---

## Work Experience

After finishing at Rhodes, I spent two years in the Memphis non-profit sector using GIS and data analytics to advocate for more equitable housing policy.

- **AmeriCorps VISTA, Neighborhood Preservation Inc.** — I wrote a *Rental Property Management Scorecard* using publicly available data to score landlords on how well they maintain their properties. This work contributed directly to the passage of a **rental license ordinance** requiring regular property inspections.
- **Memphis Mayor's Dashboard** — Worked on special projects including qualitative analysis of false security alarm triggers and local public transit improvements. 

Between the two roles, I also spent a semester teaching tenth grade geometry — a pivot I didn't expect to love as much as I did.
